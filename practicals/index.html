Lab Safety Reminder

  • Always wear lab coat and safety goggles.
  • Never taste any chemical.
  • Add acid to water (NEVER water to acid) when diluting.
  • Tie back long hair; wear closed shoes.
  • Report breakages and spills to your teacher immediately.
  • Wash hands thoroughly after every practical session.
