WAEC Math 2022

Q1. Solve: 2(x − 3) + 3(2x − 5) = 7

1
Expand the brackets: 2x − 6 + 6x − 15 = 7
2
Combine like terms: 8x − 21 = 7
3
Add 21 to both sides: 8x = 28
4
Divide both sides by 8: x = 28 / 8 = 3.5
Final Answer: x = 3.5 or 7/2
GCE Physics 2023

Q2. A stone is thrown vertically upward with velocity 30 m/s. How high does it rise? (g = 10 m/s²)

1
Identify variables: u = 30 m/s (initial), v = 0 (at max height), a = −g = −10 m/s², s = ?
2
Choose the right equation: v² = u² + 2as (no time given)
3
Substitute values: 0² = 30² + 2(−10)s
4
Solve for s: 0 = 900 − 20s → 20s = 900 → s = 45
Maximum Height = 45 m
WAEC Chemistry 2021

Q3. Calculate the percentage of nitrogen in NH₄NO₃. (N=14, H=1, O=16)

1
Calculate molar mass of NH₄NO₃: 2(N) + 4(H) + 3(O) = 2(14) + 4(1) + 3(16) = 28 + 4 + 48 = 80 g/mol
2
Mass of N in compound: 2 × 14 = 28 g
3
Apply % composition formula: %N = (mass of N / molar mass) × 100
4
Compute: %N = (28/80) × 100 = 35%
%Nitrogen = 35%
GCE Math 2024

Q4. Differentiate y = (3x² + 2)(x − 5)

1
Method: Use product rule — d(uv)/dx = u(dv/dx) + v(du/dx)
2
Let u = 3x² + 2, v = x − 5. Then du/dx = 6x, dv/dx = 1.
3
Apply rule: dy/dx = (3x² + 2)(1) + (x − 5)(6x)
4
Expand: dy/dx = 3x² + 2 + 6x² − 30x
5
Simplify: dy/dx = 9x² − 30x + 2
dy/dx = 9x² − 30x + 2
WAEC Biology 2022

Q5. (Genetics) In a cross between two heterozygous tall pea plants (Tt × Tt), what is the probability of obtaining a short offspring?

1
Set up Punnett square: Parents: Tt × Tt
2
Possible gametes: Each parent produces T or t (50% each)
3
Offspring genotypes: TT, Tt, Tt, tt → ratio 1 : 2 : 1
4
Short = tt (homozygous recessive): Only 1 out of 4 outcomes
Probability = 1/4 = 25%
GCE Physics 2022

Q6. Find equivalent resistance of three resistors (3Ω, 6Ω, 4Ω) in parallel.

1
Formula: 1/Rₑ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃
2
Substitute: 1/Rₑ = 1/3 + 1/6 + 1/4
3
LCM of 3, 6, 4 is 12: 1/Rₑ = 4/12 + 2/12 + 3/12 = 9/12
4
Invert: Rₑ = 12/9 = 4/3 Ω
Equivalent Resistance ≈ 1.33 Ω
WAEC Chemistry 2024

Q7. Balance and find the volume of CO₂ produced when 50 g of CaCO₃ decomposes at STP. (Ca=40, C=12, O=16)

1
Write balanced equation: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
2
Molar mass of CaCO₃: 40 + 12 + (3×16) = 100 g/mol
3
Moles of CaCO₃: 50/100 = 0.5 mol
4
Moles of CO₂ produced: 1:1 ratio → 0.5 mol
5
Volume at STP: V = 0.5 × 22.4 = 11.2 dm³
Volume of CO₂ = 11.2 dm³ (litres)
GCE Math 2023

Q8. Find the area enclosed between y = x² and y = 4.

1
Find intersection points: x² = 4 → x = ±2
2
Set up integral: A = ∫₋₂² (4 − x²) dx
3
Integrate: [4x − x³/3] from −2 to 2
4
Evaluate at upper: 4(2) − 8/3 = 8 − 8/3 = 16/3
5
Evaluate at lower: 4(−2) − (−8)/3 = −8 + 8/3 = −16/3
6
Subtract: 16/3 − (−16/3) = 32/3
Area = 32/3 ≈ 10.67 square units
