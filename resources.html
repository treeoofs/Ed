📅 Exam Tips & Strategies

Time Management

Allocate 1.5 minutes per multiple-choice question. Don't dwell on hard ones — flag and return.

Show Your Work

For STEM, partial marks matter. Always show steps even if you're unsure of the final answer.

Rest Before Exam

Get at least 7 hours of sleep the night before. Your brain consolidates memory while you sleep.

Eat Smart

Have a balanced meal 2 hours before. Avoid heavy or sugary foods that cause energy crashes.
