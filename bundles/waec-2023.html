Math (2023)

Math2023

1. If log₁₀ 2 = 0.3010 and log₁₀ 3 = 0.4771, find log₁₀ 12.

  • A) 1.0000
  • B) 1.0791
  • C) 1.1761
  • D) 1.2552
Solution:
  1. log 12 = log(4×3) = 2log2 + log3 = 0.6020 + 0.4771 = 1.0791
✅ B
Math2023

2. Solve: 3x + 2y = 13, 2x − y = 4. Find x.

  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 3
  • D) 4
Solution:
  1. Multiply 2nd eq by 2: 4x − 2y = 8. Add to 1st: 7x = 21 → x = 3
✅ C
Math2023

3. Find the median of: 12, 15, 8, 20, 10, 18, 9

  • A) 10
  • B) 12
  • C) 13
  • D) 15
Solution:
  1. Sorted: 8,9,10,12,15,18,20. Middle = 12
✅ B
Math2023

4. ₦5000 invested at 10% p.a. compound interest for 2 years amounts to:

  • A) ₦5500
  • B) ₦6000
  • C) ₦6050
  • D) ₦6500
Solution:
  1. A = 5000(1.1)² = 5000 × 1.21 = ₦6050
✅ C
Math2023

5. Find the determinant of [[3,2],[1,4]]

  • A) 5
  • B) 10
  • C) 12
  • D) 14
Solution:
  1. det = (3)(4) − (2)(1) = 10
✅ B
