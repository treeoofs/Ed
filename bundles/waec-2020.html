Math (2020)

Math2020

1. Area of circle r=7cm (π=22/7):

  • A) 22
  • B) 44
  • C) 154
  • D) 49
Solution:
  1. A = πr² = 22/7 × 49 = 154 cm²
✅ C
Math2020

2. Differentiate y = 4x³−6x²+5x−2:

  • A) 12x²−12x+5
  • B) 4x²−6x+5
  • C) 12x³−12x²+5
  • D) x²−x+5
Solution:
  1. Power rule: 12x²−12x+5
✅ A
Math2020

3. ∫(3x²+2x)dx:

  • A) 6x+2+C
  • B) x³+x²+C
  • C) x²+x+C
  • D) 3x³+2x²+C
Solution:
  1. x³+x²+C
✅ B
Math2020

4. Distance between (2,3) and (5,7):

  • A) 3
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 7
Solution:
  1. √(9+16) = √25 = 5
✅ C
Math2020

5. Gradient of line through (1,2) and (4,11):

  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 9
Solution:
  1. (11-2)/(4-1) = 9/3 = 3
✅ B

7. Refractive index formula:

  • A) sin i × sin r
  • B) sin i / sin r
  • C) sin r / sin i
  • D) i × r
Solution:
  1. n = sin i / sin r (Snell's law)
✅ B

8. Latent heat formula:

  • B) Q=ml

  1. For phase changes: Q = ml
