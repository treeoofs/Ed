Math2024

1. Simplify: 5x − 3(x − 4)

  • A) 2x − 4
  • B) 2x + 12
  • C) 2x − 12
  • D) 8x + 12
Solution:
  1. Expand: 5x − 3x + 12 = 2x + 12
✅ B) 2x + 12

2. Solve: 2x² + 3x − 5 = 0

  • A) x = 1 or x = −5/2
  • B) x = −1 or 5/2
  • C) x = 2 or −5
  • D) x = 5 or −2
Solution:
  1. Discriminant b²−4ac = 9 + 40 = 49
  2. x = (−3 ± 7)/4 → x = 1 or −5/2
✅ A
Math2024

3. If f(x) = 2x² − 3, find f(4).

  • A) 13
  • B) 19
  • C) 29
  • D) 32
Solution:
  1. f(4) = 2(16) − 3 = 29
✅ C) 29
Math2024

4. If cos θ = 5/13 (acute), find sin θ.

  • A) 5/12
  • B) 12/13
  • C) 13/12
  • D) 12/5
Solution:
  1. sin²θ = 1 − 25/169 = 144/169 → sin θ = 12/13
✅ B
