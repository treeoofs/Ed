Math2019

1. If sin θ = 3/5 (acute), tan θ:

  • A) 3/4
  • B) 4/5
  • C) 3/4
  • D) 5/4
Solution:
  1. Adj=4 by Pythagoras; tan = 3/4
✅ A
Math2019

2. Solve: 2(x+3) = 5x − 6:

  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 5
Solution:
  1. 2x+6=5x-6; 12=3x; x=4
✅ C

3. From 30m, elevation 45° to top of tree:

  • A) 15m
  • B) 20m
  • C) 30m
  • D) 60m

  1. tan 45° = h/30 → h = 30m
✅ C
