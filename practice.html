Q1. (Mathematics) — Solve: 3x − 5 = 16

A) x = 6
B) x = 7
C) x = 5
D) x = 21
3x − 5 = 16 → 3x = 21 → x = 7. ✅ Correct answer: B

Q2. (Physics) — A body moves with uniform velocity. Its acceleration is:

A) Maximum
B) Equal to gravity
C) Zero
D) Negative
Uniform velocity means constant velocity → no change → acceleration = 0. ✅ Correct: C

Q3. (Chemistry) — The number of moles in 22 g of CO₂ (M = 44 g/mol) is:

A) 1 mol
B) 0.5 mol
C) 2 mol
D) 0.25 mol
Moles = mass/molar mass = 22/44 = 0.5 mol. ✅ Correct: B

Q4. (Biology) — Which organelle is responsible for photosynthesis?

A) Mitochondria
B) Nucleus
C) Chloroplast
D) Ribosome
Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll which captures light energy for photosynthesis. ✅ Correct: C

Q5. (English) — Choose the correct sentence:

A) Each of the boys have done their work.
B) Each of the boys has done his work.
C) Each of the boys are doing their work.
D) Each of the boys do his work.
'Each' is singular → singular verb 'has' and singular pronoun 'his'. ✅ Correct: B

Q6. (Mathematics) — Find the value of x: log₂ x = 3

A) 6
B) 9
C) 8
D) 16
log₂ x = 3 → x = 2³ = 8. ✅ Correct: C

Q7. (Physics) — A 2 kg object accelerates at 5 m/s². The applied force is:

A) 5 N
B) 10 N
C) 20 N
D) 2.5 N
F = ma = 2 × 5 = 10 N. ✅ Correct: B

Q8. (Government) — The doctrine of separation of powers was propounded by:

A) John Locke
B) Baron de Montesquieu
C) Karl Marx
D) Aristotle
Montesquieu, in 'The Spirit of the Laws' (1748), proposed the separation of powers. ✅ Correct: B

Q9. (Economics) — When demand exceeds supply, prices tend to:

A) Fall
B) Rise
C) Remain stable
D) Become zero
Excess demand causes shortages, pushing prices upward. ✅ Correct: B

Q10. (Chemistry) — Which of these is an alkene?

A) C₂H₆
B) C₂H₄
C) C₂H₂
D) CH₄
Alkenes have formula CₙH₂ₙ. C₂H₄ matches (n=2). ✅ Correct: B (Ethene)
