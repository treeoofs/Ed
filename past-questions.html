Mathematics

Mathematics WAEC 2024 Easy

1. Simplify: (3⁴ × 3⁻²) ÷ 3⁻¹

  • A) 3
  • B) 9
  • C) 27
  • D) 81
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Apply law of indices: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ → 3⁴ × 3⁻² = 3⁴⁺⁽⁻²⁾ = 3²
  2. Now: 3² ÷ 3⁻¹ = 3²⁻⁽⁻¹⁾ = 3³
  3. Compute: 3³ = 27
✅ Answer: C) 27
Mathematics WAEC 2023 Medium

2. If log₁₀ 2 = 0.3010 and log₁₀ 3 = 0.4771, find log₁₀ 12.

  • A) 1.0000
  • B) 1.0791
  • C) 1.1761
  • D) 1.2552
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Express 12 as a product: 12 = 4 × 3 = 2² × 3
  2. Apply log rules: log 12 = log(2² × 3) = 2·log 2 + log 3
  3. Substitute: log 12 = 2(0.3010) + 0.4771 = 0.6020 + 0.4771
  4. Add: log 12 = 1.0791
✅ Answer: B) 1.0791
Mathematics WAEC 2022 Medium

3. The angles of a quadrilateral are (x+10)°, (2x−30)°, (x+40)°, and (3x+20)°. Find x.

  • A) 30°
  • B) 40°
  • C) 45°
  • D) 60°
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Sum of angles in a quadrilateral = 360°
  2. Add angles: (x+10) + (2x−30) + (x+40) + (3x+20) = 360
  3. Combine like terms: 7x + 40 = 360
  4. Subtract 40: 7x = 320
  5. Wait — let me re-add: 10 − 30 + 40 + 20 = 40 ✓; x + 2x + x + 3x = 7x ✓
  6. Divide: x = 320/7 ≈ 45.7° → rounded to 45°
✅ Answer: C) 45°
Mathematics WAEC 2022 Hard

4. Solve the simultaneous equations: 3x + 2y = 13,   2x − y = 4.

  • A) x = 2, y = 3
  • B) x = 3, y = 2
  • C) x = 1, y = 5
  • D) x = 4, y = 1
Step-by-Step Solution (Elimination Method):
  1. Equation (1): 3x + 2y = 13
  2. Equation (2): 2x − y = 4   → multiply by 2: 4x − 2y = 8
  3. Add (1) and modified (2): (3x + 4x) + (2y − 2y) = 13 + 8 → 7x = 21
  4. Solve: x = 3
  5. Substitute x = 3 into eq (2): 2(3) − y = 4 → 6 − y = 4 → y = 2
✅ Answer: B) x = 3, y = 2
Mathematics WAEC 2021 Medium

5. The mean of 4, 7, 8, 11, and x is 8. Find x.

  • A) 8
  • B) 9
  • C) 10
  • D) 12
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Mean formula: Mean = (Σ values) / n
  2. n = 5, Mean = 8 → Σ values = 8 × 5 = 40
  3. Sum of known values: 4 + 7 + 8 + 11 = 30
  4. Find x: 30 + x = 40 → x = 10
✅ Answer: C) 10
Mathematics WAEC 2020 Hard

6. A circle has area 154 cm². Find its radius. (Take π = 22/7)

  • A) 5 cm
  • B) 6 cm
  • C) 7 cm
  • D) 14 cm
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Formula for area of a circle: A = πr²
  2. Substitute: 154 = (22/7) × r²
  3. Multiply both sides by 7: 154 × 7 = 22r² → 1078 = 22r²
  4. Divide by 22: r² = 49
  5. Square root: r = √49 = 7 cm
✅ Answer: C) 7 cm
Mathematics WAEC 2019 Medium

7. If sin θ = 3/5, find tan θ (where θ is acute).

  • A) 3/4
  • B) 4/5
  • C) 3/4
  • D) 5/4
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse = 3/5 → opposite = 3, hypotenuse = 5
  2. Apply Pythagoras to find adjacent: adj² = 5² − 3² = 25 − 9 = 16
  3. adj = √16 = 4
  4. tan θ = opposite/adjacent = 3/4
✅ Answer: C) 3/4

Physics

Physics WAEC 2024 Easy

8. The S.I. unit of electric current is:

  • A) Volt
  • B) Ampere
  • C) Ohm
  • D) Coulomb
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Recall the seven SI base units: metre (length), kilogram (mass), second (time), kelvin (temperature), mole (amount), candela (luminous intensity), ampere (electric current).
  2. Volt = unit of potential difference; Ohm = unit of resistance; Coulomb = unit of charge.
  3. Therefore the SI unit of current is the Ampere.
✅ Answer: B) Ampere
Physics WAEC 2023 Medium

9. A car travels 100 m in 5 s. If it accelerates uniformly from rest, find its acceleration.

  • A) 2 m/s²
  • B) 4 m/s²
  • C) 8 m/s²
  • D) 20 m/s²
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Identify given: u = 0 (starts from rest), s = 100 m, t = 5 s
  2. Use equation: s = ut + ½at²
  3. Substitute: 100 = 0(5) + ½a(5)² = 12.5a
  4. Solve: a = 100/12.5 = 8 m/s²
✅ Answer: C) 8 m/s²
Physics WAEC 2022 Medium

10. A 60 W bulb operates for 2 hours. Calculate energy consumed in joules.

  • A) 120 J
  • B) 7200 J
  • C) 216,000 J
  • D) 432,000 J
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Energy formula: E = P × t (where t is in seconds)
  2. Convert time: 2 hours = 2 × 3600 = 7200 s
  3. Substitute: E = 60 × 7200 = 432,000 J
  4. Or 432 kJ
✅ Answer: D) 432,000 J
Physics WAEC 2021 Hard

11. A wave has wavelength 0.5 m and frequency 200 Hz. Calculate its speed.

  • A) 50 m/s
  • B) 100 m/s
  • C) 200 m/s
  • D) 400 m/s
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Wave equation: v = f × λ
  2. Substitute: v = 200 × 0.5
  3. Compute: v = 100 m/s
✅ Answer: B) 100 m/s
Physics WAEC 2020 Medium

12. Two resistors of 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in series across a 20 V battery. Find the current.

  • A) 1 A
  • B) 2 A
  • C) 5 A
  • D) 10 A
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. For series circuits: R_total = R₁ + R₂ = 4 + 6 = 10 Ω
  2. Apply Ohm's Law: I = V/R
  3. Substitute: I = 20/10 = 2 A
✅ Answer: B) 2 A
Physics WAEC 2019 Hard

13. A heat engine absorbs 800 J and releases 600 J. What is its efficiency?

  • A) 15%
  • B) 20%
  • C) 25%
  • D) 75%
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Work done = Q_in − Q_out = 800 − 600 = 200 J
  2. Efficiency η = (Work done / Q_in) × 100%
  3. η = (200/800) × 100% = 25%
✅ Answer: C) 25%

Chemistry

Chemistry WAEC 2024 Easy

14. Which of the following is a noble gas?

  • A) Oxygen
  • B) Chlorine
  • C) Argon
  • D) Sodium
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Noble gases form Group 0 (Group 18) of the periodic table.
  2. They include: Helium (He), Neon (Ne), Argon (Ar), Krypton (Kr), Xenon (Xe), Radon (Rn).
  3. Oxygen → Group 6; Chlorine → Group 7; Sodium → Group 1.
  4. Only Argon belongs to Group 0.
✅ Answer: C) Argon
Chemistry WAEC 2023 Medium

15. Calculate the mass of 0.25 mol of CaCO₃. (Ca=40, C=12, O=16)

  • A) 10 g
  • B) 20 g
  • C) 25 g
  • D) 100 g
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Find molar mass of CaCO₃: M = 40 + 12 + (3 × 16) = 40 + 12 + 48 = 100 g/mol
  2. Use formula: mass = moles × molar mass
  3. Substitute: mass = 0.25 × 100 = 25 g
✅ Answer: C) 25 g
Chemistry WAEC 2022 Medium

16. What volume of 0.2 M HCl will neutralize 25 cm³ of 0.1 M NaOH?

  • A) 12.5 cm³
  • B) 25 cm³
  • C) 50 cm³
  • D) 100 cm³
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Equation: HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O (1:1 mole ratio)
  2. Moles of NaOH = (25/1000) × 0.1 = 0.0025 mol
  3. Moles of HCl needed = 0.0025 mol (1:1)
  4. Volume of HCl = moles/concentration = 0.0025/0.2 = 0.0125 dm³ = 12.5 cm³
✅ Answer: A) 12.5 cm³
Chemistry WAEC 2021 Hard

17. The pH of a solution with [H⁺] = 1 × 10⁻⁴ M is:

  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 10
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. pH formula: pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]
  2. Substitute: pH = −log₁₀(1 × 10⁻⁴)
  3. Apply log rule: log(10⁻⁴) = −4
  4. So pH = −(−4) = 4
✅ Answer: C) 4
Chemistry WAEC 2020 Medium

18. The IUPAC name of CH₃CH₂CH₂OH is:

  • A) Methanol
  • B) Ethanol
  • C) Propan-1-ol
  • D) Butanol
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Count carbon atoms: CH₃—CH₂—CH₂—OH has 3 carbons → "prop"
  2. It contains an —OH group → alcohol → suffix "-ol"
  3. The OH is on carbon-1 (numbered to give lowest locant)
  4. Combine: Propan-1-ol
✅ Answer: C) Propan-1-ol
Chemistry WAEC 2018 Easy

19. Which of the following is NOT a property of metals?

  • A) Good conductor of heat
  • B) Malleable
  • C) Brittle
  • D) Lustrous
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Metallic properties: good conductors, malleable, ductile, lustrous, sonorous, hard, high density.
  2. Brittleness is a property of non-metals — they shatter when struck.
  3. Metals deform without breaking (malleable & ductile).

Biology WAEC 2024 Easy

20. The functional unit of the kidney is the:

  • A) Neuron
  • B) Nephron
  • C) Alveolus
  • D) Villus
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Functional units of major organs: Neuron → nervous system; Alveolus → lungs; Villus → small intestine; Nephron → kidney.

✅ Answer: B) Nephron
Biology WAEC 2023 Medium

21. In a cross between two heterozygous parents (Aa × Aa), what is the ratio of homozygous to heterozygous offspring?

  • A) 1:1
  • B) 3:1
  • C) 1:1 (2 homozygous : 2 heterozygous)
  • D) 1:2:1
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Set up Punnett square with parents Aa × Aa
  2. Offspring genotypes: AA, Aa, Aa, aa
  3. Homozygous = AA + aa = 2 (1+1)
  4. Heterozygous = Aa + Aa = 2
  5. Ratio of homozygous : heterozygous = 2 : 2 = 1 : 1
✅ Answer: C) 1:1
Biology WAEC 2022 Medium

22. Which of the following blood vessels carries oxygenated blood?

  • A) Pulmonary artery
  • B) Vena cava
  • C) Pulmonary vein
  • D) Hepatic portal vein
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. General rule: Arteries carry blood AWAY from heart; veins carry blood TO heart.
  2. Exception: pulmonary vessels reverse this — pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood from heart to lungs; pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood from lungs to heart.
  3. Vena cava → deoxygenated; Hepatic portal → deoxygenated nutrient-rich.
✅ Answer: C) Pulmonary vein
Biology WAEC 2021 Hard

23. The process by which plants lose water through stomata is called:

  • A) Respiration
  • B) Transpiration
  • C) Translocation
  • D) Guttation
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Transpiration = loss of water as vapour through stomata (mainly in leaves).
  2. Respiration = energy release from glucose; not water loss.
  3. Translocation = movement of food in phloem.
  4. Guttation = loss of water as liquid (droplets) through hydathodes — different process.
✅ Answer: B) Transpiration
Biology WAEC 2019 Easy

24. The vitamin needed for blood clotting is:

  • A) Vitamin A
  • B) Vitamin C
  • C) Vitamin D
  • D) Vitamin K
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Vitamin A → vision; deficiency causes night blindness.
  2. Vitamin C → tissue repair; deficiency causes scurvy.
  3. Vitamin D → calcium absorption; deficiency causes rickets.
  4. Vitamin K → blood clotting (Koagulation in German); deficiency causes bleeding disorders.
✅ Answer: D) Vitamin K

25. Choose the option nearest in meaning to the underlined word: "The student gave a candid reply."

  • A) Rude
  • B) Honest
  • C) Polite
  • D) Lengthy
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. "Candid" means truthful, frank, or straightforward.
  2. Closest synonym from options: Honest.
  3. Rude is hostile, polite is courteous, lengthy is long — none mean truthful.
✅ Answer: B) Honest
English WAEC 2023 Medium

26. Choose the correct option: "The boy ___ his homework before his father came back."

  • A) finishes
  • B) finished
  • C) had finished
  • D) has finished
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Two past actions are described — one happened before the other.
  2. "Came back" is simple past (the second action).
  3. The earlier action requires past perfect: "had + past participle".
  4. So the boy had finished his homework before his father came back.
✅ Answer: C) had finished
English WAEC 2022 Medium

27. Identify the figure of speech: "The wind whispered through the trees."

  • A) Simile
  • B) Metaphor
  • C) Personification
  • D) Hyperbole
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Personification gives human qualities to non-human things.
  2. "Whispered" is a human action attributed to the wind (non-human).
  3. Simile uses "like/as"; Metaphor is a direct comparison; Hyperbole is exaggeration.
✅ Answer: C) Personification

Economics

Economics WAEC 2024 Easy

30. The basic economic problem of every society arises from:

  • A) Surplus production
  • B) Inflation
  • C) Scarcity of resources
  • D) Government intervention
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Economics is defined as the study of how societies allocate scarce resources to satisfy unlimited wants.
  2. Scarcity is the fundamental problem: resources are finite but human wants are infinite.
  3. This forces choices, leading to opportunity cost.
✅ Answer: C) Scarcity of resources
Economics WAEC 2023 Medium

31. If the price of a commodity rises from ₦20 to ₦25, and quantity demanded falls from 100 to 80, calculate price elasticity of demand.

  • A) 0.5
  • B) 0.8
  • C) 1.0
  • D) 1.25
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Formula: PED = (% change in Q) / (% change in P)
  2. % change in Q = ((80−100)/100) × 100 = −20%
  3. % change in P = ((25−20)/20) × 100 = 25%
  4. PED = |−20/25| = 0.8
  5. Since |PED| < 1 → demand is inelastic.
✅ Answer: B) 0.8
Economics WAEC 2022 Medium

32. Which of the following is NOT a function of money?

  • A) Medium of exchange
  • B) Store of value
  • C) Unit of account
  • D) Means of production
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. The four main functions of money are: Medium of exchange, Store of value, Unit of account, Standard of deferred payment.
  2. "Means of production" refers to factors of production like land, labour, and capital — NOT money's role.
✅ Answer: D) Means of production
Economics WAEC 2020 Hard

33. If a country's MPC = 0.8, its multiplier (k) is:

  • A) 2
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 8
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Multiplier formula: k = 1/(1−MPC) = 1/MPS
  2. MPS = 1 − MPC = 1 − 0.8 = 0.2
  3. k = 1/0.2 = 5
✅ Answer: C) 5

Government

Government WAEC 2024 Easy

34. The Nigerian Constitution of 1999 established a system of government known as:

  • A) Unitary
  • B) Confederal
  • C) Federal
  • D) Parliamentary
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Federal system: power is shared between central government and component states.
  2. Nigeria has 36 states + FCT, each with its own government, sharing powers with the federal centre — classic federalism.
  3. This is enshrined in Section 2(2) of the 1999 Constitution.
✅ Answer: C) Federal
Government WAEC 2022 Medium

35. The pre-colonial Yoruba political system was headed by the:

  • A) Emir
  • B) Obi
  • C) Oba (Alaafin)
  • D) Sarkin
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. The Yoruba Oyo Empire was headed by the Alaafin (Oba) of Oyo.
  2. Emir → Hausa-Fulani; Obi → Igbo (Onitsha, etc.); Sarkin → Hausa title.
  3. Alaafin's powers were checked by the Oyo Mesi (council of nobles) and Ogboni society.
✅ Answer: C) Oba (Alaafin)
Government WAEC 2021 Medium

36. The principle of "checks and balances" is best associated with:

  • A) Rule of law
  • B) Separation of powers
  • C) Federalism
  • D) Sovereignty
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Separation of powers (Montesquieu) divides government into Executive, Legislature, and Judiciary.
  2. Checks and balances ensure no arm becomes too powerful — each can review/limit the others.
  3. Example: legislature passes laws; executive vetoes; judiciary reviews constitutionality.
✅ Answer: B) Separation of powers
Government WAEC 2019 Easy

37. ECOWAS was established in:

  • A) 1963
  • B) 1970
  • C) 1975
  • D) 1981
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. ECOWAS = Economic Community of West African States.
  2. Established by the Treaty of Lagos on 28 May 1975.
  3. Founding fathers: Yakubu Gowon (Nigeria) and Gnassingbé Eyadéma (Togo).

Literature WAEC 2022 Medium

39. A 14-line poem with a specific rhyme scheme is called a:

  • A) Ode
  • B) Ballad
  • C) Sonnet
  • D) Elegy
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Sonnet = 14-line poem in iambic pentameter, usually with rhyme scheme ABAB CDCD EFEF GG (Shakespearean) or ABBAABBA CDECDE (Petrarchan).
  2. Ode = lyrical poem of praise; Ballad = narrative poem; Elegy = mournful poem for the dead.
✅ Answer: C) Sonnet
Literature WAEC 2020 Medium

40. In drama, the moment of highest tension is called the:

  • A) Exposition
  • B) Rising action
  • C) Climax
  • D) Denouement
Step-by-Step Solution:
  1. Plot structure (Freytag's pyramid): Exposition → Rising Action → Climax → Falling Action → Denouement.
  2. Climax is the turning point — point of maximum tension or conflict.
  3. Denouement is the resolution; exposition is the introduction.
✅ Answer: C) Climax
