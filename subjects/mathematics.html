1. Algebra & Linear Equations

Algebra deals with letters and symbols representing numbers in expressions and equations. A linear equation has variables raised to the power 1 only.

General Form

ax + b = 0, where a ≠ 0

Solving Linear Equations — Step by Step

Example: Solve 3x + 7 = 22

Step 1 — Subtract 7 from both sides: 3x + 7 − 7 = 22 − 7 → 3x = 15
Step 2 — Divide both sides by 3: x = 15 ÷ 3
Step 3 — ✅ x = 5

Simultaneous Equations (Elimination Method)

Example: Solve 2x + y = 11 and x − y = 1

Step 1 — Add the two equations to eliminate y:
(2x + y) + (x − y) = 11 + 1 → 3x = 12
Step 2 — x = 4
Step 3 — Substitute x = 4 into x − y = 1: 4 − y = 1 → y = 3
✅ Final: x = 4, y = 3

2. Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is one in which the highest power of the variable is 2.

ax² + bx + c = 0 (a ≠ 0)

Methods of Solving

1. Factorization   2. Completing the square   3. Quadratic formula

Quadratic Formula

x = [ −b ± √(b² − 4ac) ] / 2a
Example (WAEC Past Question): Solve x² − 7x + 12 = 0

Step 1 — Identify a = 1, b = −7, c = 12
Step 2 — Find two numbers whose product = ac = 12 and sum = b = −7. They are −3 and −4.
Step 3 — Rewrite middle term: x² − 3x − 4x + 12 = 0
Step 4 — Group: x(x − 3) − 4(x − 3) = 0
Step 5 — Factor: (x − 3)(x − 4) = 0
Step 6 — Apply zero-product property: x = 3 or x = 4
✅ Final: x = 3 or x = 4

3. Indices & Logarithms

Laws of Indices

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ a⁰ = 1 a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ

Logarithms

If aˣ = N, then logaN = x

Example: Simplify log 8 + log 125 (base 10)

Step 1 — Use rule: log A + log B = log(A × B)
Step 2 — log(8 × 125) = log 1000
Step 3 — log 1000 = log 10³ = 3
✅ Answer: 3

4. Geometry

Study of shapes, sizes, and properties of figures.

Key Theorems

  • Sum of angles in a triangle = 180°
  • Sum of angles in a quadrilateral = 360°
  • Vertically opposite angles are equal
  • Pythagoras: a² + b² = c² (right-angled triangle)
Example: A right triangle has legs 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

Step 1 — Apply Pythagoras: c² = a² + b²
Step 2 — c² = 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100
Step 3 — c = √100 = 10
✅ Hypotenuse = 10 cm

5. Trigonometry

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Example: A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall, making an angle of 60° with the ground. How high does it reach?

Step 1 — Identify: hypotenuse = 10 m, angle = 60°, height = opposite side
Step 2 — Use sin: sin 60° = height / 10
Step 3 — sin 60° = √3/2 ≈ 0.866
Step 4 — height = 10 × 0.866 = 8.66 m
✅ Height ≈ 8.66 m

6. Calculus (Differentiation & Integration)

Differentiation Rule

d/dx (xⁿ) = n · xⁿ⁻¹
Example: Differentiate y = 3x⁴ − 5x² + 7

Step 1 — Apply power rule term-by-term
Step 2 — dy/dx of 3x⁴ = 4 · 3x³ = 12x³
Step 3 — dy/dx of −5x² = 2 · (−5)x = −10x
Step 4 — dy/dx of 7 (constant) = 0
✅ dy/dx = 12x³ − 10x

Integration Rule

∫ xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹ / (n+1) + C (n ≠ −1)
Example: Find ∫ (4x³ + 6x) dx

Step 1 — Integrate term-by-term
Step 2 — ∫4x³ dx = 4 · x⁴/4 = x⁴
Step 3 — ∫6x dx = 6 · x²/2 = 3x²
✅ Answer = x⁴ + 3x² + C

7. Statistics & Probability

Mean = Σx / n Probability = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes
Example: Find the mean of: 4, 8, 6, 5, 7

Step 1 — Sum: 4+8+6+5+7 = 30
Step 2 — Count: n = 5
Step 3 — Mean = 30 / 5 = 6
✅ Mean = 6
Probability Example: A bag contains 3 red and 5 blue balls. Probability of picking a red ball?

Step 1 — Total balls = 3 + 5 = 8
Step 2 — Favourable (red) = 3
Step 3 — P(red) = 3/8
✅ P(red) = 3/8 = 0.375

8. Mensuration

Area of circle = πr² Volume of cylinder = πr²h Volume of cone = (1/3)πr²h Volume of sphere = (4/3)πr³
Example: Find the volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. (Take π = 22/7)

Step 1 — Formula: V = πr²h
Step 2 — V = (22/7) × 7² × 10
Step 3 — V = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 22 × 7 × 10
✅ V = 1540 cm³
