1. Essay Writing

Types: Narrative, Descriptive, Argumentative, Expository, Article.

Structure

  • Introduction — hook, background, thesis
  • Body — 3-4 paragraphs, one idea each, with evidence
  • Conclusion — restate thesis, summarise, closing thought
Tip: Use connectives — However, Furthermore, In contrast, As a result, In conclusion — to link ideas smoothly.

2. Comprehension

Strategy:

  1. Skim the passage for main idea.
  2. Read questions carefully.
  3. Re-read passage for details.
  4. Answer in your own words where possible.
  5. Use evidence from the text.

3. Lexis & Structure

Topics: synonyms, antonyms, idioms, concord (subject-verb agreement), tense, prepositions.

Example: Choose the correct option:
"Neither John nor his brothers ___ at home."
(a) is  (b) are  (c) was

Rule: When subjects are joined by 'neither...nor', verb agrees with the subject closest to it.
"Brothers" (plural) is closest, so verb is plural.
✅ Answer: (b) are

4. Oral English

Phonetics: vowels (/i:/, /ɪ/, /e/, /æ/, /ʌ/, /ɑ:/, /ɒ/, /ɔ:/, /ʊ/, /u:/, /ɜ:/, /ə/) and diphthongs (/eɪ/, /aɪ/, /ɔɪ/, /aʊ/, /əʊ/, /ɪə/, /eə/, /ʊə/).

Q: Which word has the same vowel sound as 'sheep'?
(a) ship  (b) seat  (c) set
'Sheep' has /i:/ (long ee). 'Seat' also has /i:/.
✅ Answer: (b) seat

5. Summary Writing

Aim: convey the main idea in fewer words. Don't copy verbatim — paraphrase.

6. Letter Writing

Three types: Formal (official), Semi-formal, Informal (friendly).

Formal letter format: Sender's address, date, receiver's address, salutation (Dear Sir/Madam), heading, body (3 paragraphs), close (Yours faithfully), signature, name in capitals.
