1. Cell Biology

The cell is the basic unit of life. Cells can be plant or animal.

Animal vs Plant Cell

  • Animal cell: nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria, ribosomes
  • Plant cell: all of the above PLUS cell wall, large vacuole, chloroplasts
Q: State three differences between plant and animal cells.

Step 1 — Plant cells have a cell wall; animal cells don't.
Step 2 — Plant cells have chloroplasts; animal cells don't.
Step 3 — Plant cells have a large central vacuole; animal cells have small or no vacuoles.
✅ Three clear differences identified.

2. Nutrition & Digestion

Major food classes: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water, roughage.

Enzymes & Digestion

  • Amylase: starch → maltose
  • Pepsin (in stomach): protein → peptides
  • Lipase: fat → fatty acids + glycerol

3. Respiration

Aerobic respiration releases energy from glucose using oxygen.

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ENERGY (ATP)

Anaerobic respiration occurs without oxygen: glucose → lactic acid (in muscles) or alcohol + CO₂ (in yeast).

4. Photosynthesis

6CO₂ + 6H₂O →(sunlight, chlorophyll)→ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Factors affecting rate: light intensity, CO₂ concentration, temperature, chlorophyll.

5. Genetics

Mendel's laws explain inheritance.

Example (Monohybrid Cross): A pure tall plant (TT) crosses with a pure short plant (tt). Find F₁ and F₂ ratios.

Step 1 — Parental: TT × tt → F₁ all Tt (tall, heterozygous)
Step 2 — F₁ self-cross: Tt × Tt
Step 3 — Punnett square: TT, Tt, Tt, tt
Step 4 — Genotype ratio: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt
Step 5 — Phenotype ratio: 3 Tall : 1 Short
✅ F₂ Phenotype = 3:1

6. Ecology

Food chain: producers → primary consumers → secondary consumers → tertiary consumers.

Example: Grass → grasshopper → frog → snake → hawk

Energy decreases as we move up trophic levels (10% rule).

7. Reproduction

Asexual: one parent, identical offspring (e.g., binary fission, budding).
Sexual: two parents, gamete fusion, genetic variation.

In humans: sperm (23 chromosomes) + ovum (23 chromosomes) → zygote (46 chromosomes).
